Top Liberian Artists

JZyNo

Top Liberian Afro artist from Monrovia's slums, known for global hits like "Butta My Bread" and "Kpan Kpan Me". Rags-to-riches star.[web:1][web:11][web:14]

Kobazzie

Born 1989 in Voinjama, Afropop sensation with hits "Bounce" (ft. Davido) and "Everyday" (ft. Stonebwoy). Multiple award winner.[page:2][web:12]

Takun J

Pioneer of Hipco genre (born 1981), activist rapper with albums *The Time* (2007) and *My Way* (2012). Songs like "Police Man" tackle corruption.[page:1][web:8]

Eric Geso

Award-winning singer-songwriter, T/G Artist of the Year 2022. Known for tracks like "Kekeleh" ft. Natif.[web:1][web:13][web:16]
